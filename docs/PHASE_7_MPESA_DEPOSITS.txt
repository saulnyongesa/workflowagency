2. IMPORTANT RULES
------------------

- Browser/STK request success does not credit the wallet.
- Wallet credit happens only after successful callback processing.
- M-Pesa receipt numbers are unique.
- Duplicate callbacks are accepted but do not double-credit.
- M-Pesa credentials must be stored in environment variables.
- Callback URLs must be public HTTPS URLs in production.
