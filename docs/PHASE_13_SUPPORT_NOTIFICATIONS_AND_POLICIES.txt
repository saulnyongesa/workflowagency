PHASE 13 - SUPPORT, NOTIFICATIONS, AND POLICIES
===============================================

Status: Implemented foundation
Date: 2026-06-29


1. GOAL
-------

Phase 13 makes the system easier to support and explain. Users can view FAQs, published policies, announcements, and create support tickets. Staff can review tickets and reply from a support queue.


2. IMPLEMENTED FILES
--------------------

- `support/models.py`
  - `Announcement`
  - `FAQ`
  - `PolicyPage`
  - `SupportTicket`
  - `SupportTicketReply`
- `support/forms.py`
  - user ticket form
  - reply form
  - staff update form
- `support/views.py`
  - support center
  - ticket list/create/detail
  - staff ticket queue
  - policy list/detail
  - FAQ list
- `support/admin.py`
- `support/urls.py`
- `templates/support/`
- `support/tests.py`


3. USER FLOW
------------

1. User opens Support.
2. User sees announcements, FAQs, policy links, and recent tickets.
3. User creates a ticket with subject, category, priority, contact details, and message.
4. User can view ticket conversation and reply.
5. Staff reply moves the ticket to waiting for user.
6. User reply moves it back to waiting for staff.


4. STAFF FLOW
-------------

Staff can:

- View open support queue.
- Filter by status.
- Open ticket detail.
- Reply to the user.
- Assign ticket to a staff user.
- Add admin notes.
- Mark ticket resolved or closed.


5. POLICY PAGES
---------------

Admin can publish:

- Terms of service.
- Privacy policy.
- Withdrawal policy.
- Job reward policy.
- Referral policy.
- Support policy.
- Other policy pages.

Policy pages are public so users can review rules before and after registration.


6. ANNOUNCEMENTS
----------------

Announcements provide a lightweight notification foundation.

Supported audiences:

- all users
- active users
- staff only

Future email/SMS/push notification hooks can use this model as a source of publishable notices.
